# Personal Knowledge Boundary

SIMS Blog Manager distribution artifacts must not contain real tenant/blog names, tenant URLs, owner-specific article IDs, or owner-only operational learning history.

Allowed in the product repository:
- synthetic examples (`example.com`, sample site names, synthetic Article IDs);
- generalized regression fixtures;
- canonical machine contracts and reusable editorial rules.

Owner/site/article-specific information belongs outside the distributable repository in Personal Editorial Knowledge or current operational Evidence.
