# SIMS Blog Manager v5.14.11

- User-facing SIMS Doctor is renamed to SIMS Article Doctor.
- Site-wide diagnosis is presented as SIMS Site Doctor.
- Article list now offers Article Doctor diagnosis for the checked article.
- Direct Doctor entry from Improvement Effect is removed from the Doctor menu; observation-completion flow remains standard.
- Detailed diagnosis candidates remain the dedicated Article Doctor route.
- Duplicate legacy individual-diagnosis menu entries are removed.
- Detailed-candidate command requires the detailed-candidate sheet to be active, preventing cross-sheet checkbox misinterpretation.
- Internal sbmDoctor functions and physical sheet names remain unchanged for compatibility.
