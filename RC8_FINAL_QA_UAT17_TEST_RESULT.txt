RC8 Final QA-UAT17

Observed real-sheet failures from UAT16:
- Improvement Trend / History: ~10 seconds (improved, still not instant).
- Today's Improvements: ~2 minutes.
- Completed row remained.
- No replacement candidate appeared.
- Old checkbox validations remained in unused columns.

UAT17 change:
- Today's Improvements now uses a dedicated fast refresh path.
- Reads only URL + Work State columns from Article Management for completion blocking.
- Treats visible '完了' rows as completed even before relying on Article Management.
- Reuses saved candidates; performs candidate selection only when the queue is short.
- Rebuilds the visible queue after filtering, so completed rows disappear and vacancies refill.
- Does not run Home refresh, Doctor reconciliation, full Article Management writeback,
  full main-query repair, or full-sheet restyling when merely opening Today's Improvements.
- Clears old data validations across the whole Today sheet to remove ghost checkboxes.
